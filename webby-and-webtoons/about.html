📖 How to Pick Your First Webtoon

  1. Start with a completed series — no waiting between episodes.
  2. Check the episode count. Under 100 is a weekend; over 200 is a commitment.
  3. Read the first three episodes before deciding. If it hasn't hooked you, let it go.
  4. Match the vibe to your mood — cozy for wind-downs, tense for binges.
  5. Don't force it. Dropping a series isn't failure, it's curation.
